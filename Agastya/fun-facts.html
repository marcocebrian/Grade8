Eight surprising facts

Hover on a computer, or tap on a phone, to reveal the secret behind each number.

27,600
km per hour
What's that? →
💨

That's how fast satellites zoom around Earth — about 23 times the speed of sound! Faster than anything you've ever ridden.

16
sunrises every day
What's that? →
🌅

Astronauts on the Space Station see the Sun come up about 16 times every single day! That's a lot of breakfasts.

0
sound in space
What's that? →
🤫

Space is almost completely silent, because there's no air to carry sound. Even a giant explosion would make no noise at all.

1.3s
signal to Earth
What's that? →
⚡

Radio signals travel at the speed of light. A message from the ISS reaches the ground in about 1.3 seconds — faster than you can blink twice.

58 cm
the first satellite
What's that? →
⚽

Sputnik 1 was about the size of a beach ball. The first machine ever sent to space was tiny!

109 m
Space Station width
What's that? →
🏟️

The International Space Station is as long as a whole football field! Astronauts live and work inside it together.

~90
minutes per orbit
What's that? →
⏱️

A satellite in low orbit goes all the way around the Earth in about 90 minutes — that's faster than a long film.

∞
no fuel to keep moving
What's that? →
🔄

In space there's no air to slow it down, so a satellite can keep gliding on its orbit for years without using fuel.

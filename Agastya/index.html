Why should you care?

Human-made satellites are like super-eyes in the sky

A satellite is a machine that people build and launch into space, where it circles the Earth. Up there, it can look at the ENTIRE planet at once — something nobody standing on the ground can do. That's why we use them for so many jobs.

  • ✓Telecom & internet: phone calls, texts, video and broadband jump up to space and back down — even in the middle of the ocean.
  • ✓TV & radio: one broadcast satellite sends the same channel to millions of homes at once.
  • ✓Maps & GPS: the blue dot on a phone map comes from navigation satellites.
  • ✓Weather: they spot storms and hurricanes days before they arrive.
  • ✓Military & defence: countries use their own satellites to keep watch and stay in touch.
  • ✓Science: they watch oceans, forests, ice, and even far-away galaxies.
